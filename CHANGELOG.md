# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed

- **Shared links and hosted files now expire one hour after they were made.** They were deleted only when a later link was made, so with no new uploads a link (`GET /api/external/{token}`, `GET /api/share/{token}`) kept working after its hour; an expired one now answers "expired" and is deleted when it is opened. `backend/tmp/`, where they are kept, is git-ignored, and the test files committed there are removed.
- **Send to NORMA, Send to Cytoscape and the STRING card's _Connect: Directly_ were blocked on the public server, and Bootstrap's small icons (select arrows, ticks) were missing there.** The content-security policy in `nginx/nginx.conf` allowed no connections to other sites and no `data:` images; it now allows `data:` and `blob:` images, and connections to `https:` sites and to Cytoscape on the user's own computer (`localhost`), while scripts still load only from Arena3D itself.
- Scene & Camera → _Show Navigator (2D)_ could seem to do nothing: Performance → _Navigator (2D) refresh_ set to _Off_ (the _Fast_ preset does) hid the navigator and stopped it drawing while the box stayed ticked, so unticking changed nothing and ticking brought back an empty frame. The two now agree: the refresh _Off_ unticks the box, ticking it turns the refresh back on (every frame) and draws at once, and other Performance changes leave the navigator as the box says.
- The theme picker in the top bar was greyed out until a network was loaded, so it seemed not to work; it now works from the start, and the network loaded next takes the chosen theme — its default colours, labels, edges and floors, which kept the dark theme's look before (white labels and pale colours on a light background).
- In the convolutional neural network example, the _Dense_ and _Output_ nodes ran past the top and bottom of their floors; those floors are now wide enough for their columns. The transformer example's attention heads are centred, so no node touches the edge of its floor. The example generator (`frontend/scripts/neural_network_examples.py`) now refuses to write a node that does not fit on its floor, and an e2e test checks every node of the six neural network examples.
- The scene did not redraw when a layout finished: the edges between layers and the labels stayed where the nodes had been until the scene was moved. Every layout, arrangement, undo and redo now redraws at once, a light check every few frames redraws whatever moved nodes or layers without saying so, and hulls and fog clouds follow on the next frame.
- A hive plot and 3D containers did not give way to each other (switching between a hive plot and spheres left a mix of both). A hive plot now sets cubes, spheres and fog clouds aside while it is on, and leaving it (another arrangement or undo) brings them back with the nodes where they were; choosing a 3D container during a hive plot ends the plot first, as its own undo step. Parallel and Zig Zag space 3D volumes so they no longer overlap, and fit the view when that makes the row wider than the window.
- Group Layout (NORMA-2.0) after clustering: _Layers_ as groups (always laid out in 3D) failed with "Reingold-Tilford has no 3D version" and moved nothing, for every strategy, as did any 2D-only algorithm in sphere, cube or cloud layers; _Group gravity_ ran with Reingold-Tilford and the other algorithms that ignore edge weights, so _Force strength_ did nothing and the groups were not pulled together; _Virtual node per group_ with Circle, Grid or Random ignored the groups; and in _Single network_ mode each stacked layer was laid out on its own, so a community spanning layers was scattered. The card now offers only what the strategy can run — 3D algorithms in 3D, Fruchterman-Reingold, Kamada-Kawai or DrL for gravity, no edge-blind algorithm for virtual nodes — switches a ruled-out choice to one that works and says why, the layout service refuses the same combinations with the reason, and Single network mode lays the whole network out as one graph.
- A Shift + Drag that started on a floor moved that layer by the first mouse step before selecting (the layer drag saw the move first); Shift + Drag now never grabs a floor.
- A hive plot hid the scene's own axes only if the Show Scene Coord System box was ticked, but a newly loaded network draws them whatever the box says, so a second hive plot in a row left them crossing its centre; it now goes by what is on screen.
- In a flat hive plot, links between opposite axes (e.g. up and down with four axes) ran straight through the centre, on top of the axes; they now curve round the centre.
- Arranging layers as a chord diagram fitted the view as an undo step of its own, recorded when the camera animation ended, so one Undo took back either the view or the arrangement depending on timing; the fit is now part of the arrangement's step (as for the hive plot), and the e2e test that passed only by that timing checks the real result.
- Navigation Controls ignored quick clicks (the first step waited 70 ms, so a click released sooner did nothing) and a held button stopped at the slightest mouse movement, or kept going when released outside it. Every button now steps on press; held down it repeats until released anywhere, through small movements, after a 300 ms pause, so a click stays one step. The spread/pull buttons repeat too, each press is one undo step, the buttons never select text, and they work with touch and pen.
- OmniPath fetched nothing for gene names typed in lower case, for UniProt IDs and for a single protein, and crashed (HTTP 500) for organisms OmniPath does not cover; names are sent as OmniPath expects them (TP53 for human, Trp53 for mouse and rat), UniProt IDs match, and other organisms get a clear message.
- IntAct crashed (HTTP 500) whenever interaction partners were included — names such as `"ccsb isoform id: ndn_2"` broke the import's table — and could not match UniProt IDs among the proteins; quotes are dropped from imported names (all importers), and IntAct matches gene names or any identifier.
- When nothing is found among the typed proteins, IntAct and OmniPath now say how many interactions with other partners exist and which checkbox includes them.
- The report tabs inside Data Tables were laid out as drawers of their own (the drawer rules matched every `.tab-pane`), which cut the tables on wide drawers; the rules now apply to the top-level panels only.

### Security

- **A security review, and fixes.** Colours from sessions, attribute files and shared links are checked as CSS colours wherever they are used (a colour such as `#fff"><img onerror=…>` could break out of the Layer selection swatch and the Plots charts, and `red;position:fixed…` could add CSS), in the browser and when the server normalises a session; node links in sessions must be web or mail links. Hand-offs to other sites open without a link back to Arena3D's tab (`noopener`), and Data Tables CSV cells that start with `= + - @` are quoted so spreadsheets do not run them as formulas. On the server: a request names each layer, channel, node or group at most once and within the limits (a layer named a thousand times multiplied the work); the inter-layer misalignment counts one copy per layer and label (a malformed request could exhaust memory); layouts, clustering and metrics run in the worker pool under a time limit (`ARENA_COMPUTE_SECONDS`, 300 s), and node metrics no longer hold the server while igraph runs; uploads are read at most to the size limit, counted as they stream in when they have no Content-Length, parsed off the event loop, and refused by row count before they are parsed; group layouts count their group hubs against the quadratic-layout limit and take at most 2,000 groups and 12 per node; weighted 3D Kamada-Kawai, which can crash igraph, is refused; stored sessions and hosted files are capped in bytes as well as number (`ARENA_MAX_STORE_BYTES`, 1 GB); database imports run a few at a time (`ARENA_DB_CONCURRENCY`, 8); malformed GML, CX2 and session names are clean 400 errors; share-file names are ASCII; the IntAct query and the STRING address (also with Connect: Directly) are checked. The nginx image sends `X-Content-Type-Options`, `Referrer-Policy`, `X-Frame-Options` and `frame-ancestors`, and hides its version. `npm audit`, `pip-audit` and CodeQL report nothing open.
- Quadratic layouts (Kamada-Kawai, MDS, Graphopt, Sugiyama) are limited to 2,000 nodes per layout (`ARENA_MAX_QUADRATIC_NODES`), and layouts, clustering, metrics and statistics run off the event loop, so one request cannot stall or exhaust the service.
- Shared exports (`GET /api/share/{token}`) are served as downloads in a sandbox (`Content-Disposition: attachment`, a `sandbox` content-security policy, `nosniff`), never as pages of the application's origin.
- Links answered by a NORMA server and the Flame address must be web addresses (`http(s)`); node links passed to `POST /api/open` must be `http(s)` or `mailto` links.
- Database imports have a time budget for the whole request (`ARENA_DB_TOTAL_SECONDS`, 150 s by default).
- CodeQL code-scanning alerts on the pull request fixed: the stored-session and share-file paths are normalised and must sit directly in the temporary folder (`POST /api/external`, `GET /api/external/{token}`, `/api/share`; py/path-injection), a colour in the enrichment table is escaped like the text beside it (js/xss-through-dom), and a test recognises STRING by the URL's host instead of a substring (py/incomplete-url-substring-sanitization).
- Dependencies past six Dependabot alerts (all development-only): httpx2 2.10.0 → 2.13.1 (GHSA-8xx6-hgc6-gc2m, high: unbounded memory while decompressing streamed responses; GHSA-pf96-p4fj-6566 and GHSA-h4x7-gw46-3wm6, medium: conflicting `Content-Length`/`Transfer-Encoding` headers, multipart header injection), vitest and @vitest/mocker 4.1.10 → 4.1.12 (GHSA-82fw-gwwq-j7x9, medium: file read through a redirect mock) and source-map-js 1.2.1 → 1.2.2, through vite's postcss (GHSA-68fv-2mgg-jv7q, high: event-loop denial of service from indexed source-map section offsets). The two direct dependencies' minimum versions are raised so a re-lock cannot fall back to a vulnerable release: `httpx2>=2.12.0` in `backend/pyproject.toml`, `vitest ^4.1.11` in `frontend/package.json`.

### Added

- **A video tour, on Home and in Help.** The four-minute _Arena3D 3.0 tour_ (YouTube, with captions) has a section of its own on Home, under the numbers, and opens Help → _Getting Started_, each with its 13 chapters: a click on a chapter starts the video there. The pages show a still from the tour (`frontend/public/images/video/tour.jpg`) with a play button, and YouTube's player, in its privacy-enhanced mode (`youtube-nocookie.com`), loads only when it is pressed, so the pages still load nothing from other sites; _Privacy & Data_ says so, and the content-security policy in `nginx/nginx.conf` allows that player as a frame (`frame-src`).
- **A new example, four layers in glowing spheres, on Home.** _Layers in 3D spheres_ takes the place of _GPCR signalling_ among Home's six example cards (GPCR signalling stays in the list of all examples and in Help): the four weighted layers, each a glowing sphere (_Bubble_) with its nodes laid out in 3D inside, set in a row with the links between them bundled (`frontend/public/data/figure2A_spheres.json`). It is also under _Layer geometry and 3D_ in the examples list and on Help → _Examples_.
- **An API menu, and a better-documented REST API.** _API_ in the top bar, before Help, opens a page on Arena3D's REST API: the base URL, the version and the server's limits (read from `GET /api/config`), code to copy in curl, Python, R and JavaScript that opens a two-layer network as a link, and the interactive Swagger UI (bundled with the app as `swagger-ui-dist` 5.33.1 and loaded the first time the page opens, so no CDN is contacted; _Try it out_ calls this server). Every JSON endpoint now documents a worked example — one small two-layer gene network — that runs as it is, and a test posts each example to its endpoint so the documentation cannot drift. `GET /api/config` also gives the version, the node, upload and time limits, links to the docs, and the layout, 3D layout and clustering algorithm names. Help → API describes the menu.
- **Privacy & Data, and "free and open to all, no login" on Home and About.** Help has a new _Privacy & Data_ page (Reference group): Arena3D is free and open to all users, with no login, registration or e-mail address; networks stay in the browser tab and are processed on the server but not stored; only the links asked for (hosted exports and API links) are kept, and they are deleted after one hour; the server logs requests, not file contents; there are no cookies, analytics or tracking, and local storage holds interface preferences only; which outside service each import, enrichment and hand-off contacts, only when clicked; and how to run Arena3D privately with Docker. The Home page says _Free and open to all users · no login required_ and shows the licence (GNU AGPL v3) and a _Privacy & data_ link under its buttons; About says the same and has a _Privacy & data_ card; the footer has a _Privacy & data_ link and now sits clear of an open drawer instead of running under it. Getting Started has a _Browsers_ section (current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, with WebGL 2), and the page has a description for search engines.

- **More 3D layer containers, nodes inside or on their surface, and a container look.** Edit → Layers → _Shape & container_ groups the layer shapes as _Flat_ (Floor, None), _Solid_ (Cube, Sphere and nine new ones) and _Soft_ (Fog cloud and the new Bubble). The new shapes: _Cylinder_ (an upright column), _Cone_ and _Pyramid_ (a hierarchy: nodes in up to five tiers by degree, most at the wide base, the hubs at the top), _Dome_ (a hemisphere over a round base), _Ellipsoid_ (an upright egg for elongated layouts), _Torus_ (a ring standing across the floor, facing the viewer, for cycles and periodic data: the floor's layout wraps round the ring), _Hexagonal prism_ (a honeycomb cell), _Geodesic sphere_ (a faceted globe), _Disc stack_ (five thin plates, nodes sorted by degree into slices) and _Bubble_ (a soft sphere with a glowing rim and no outline). _Nodes: Inside / On the surface_ puts a layer's nodes in the volume, clear of its walls, or on its surface (a cylinder's wall, the rim of each tier). A layer already in 3D keeps its layout when it changes shape — the nodes move from the old shape into the new one in the same undo step — and later layouts, group layouts and refits stay inside the shape; placement is the same every time. The container look adds an _Outline_ (on or off, with its own colour), a _Wireframe_ cage, a _Glow_ (a rim light, like glass) and _Floor under it_ to the colour and opacity, for the selected layers or all, undoable and kept in sessions (older sessions load as before). Each shape fits the room a cube had, so layers are spaced as before, and the axial ones stand upright in the Parallel arrangement. A layer's own 3D layout now fills its own container (it was spread over the box of the widest layout, which left small layers in a corner). Hovered and selected layers' containers now take the highlight colour, as their floors do. Layers with the same container share one drawing (geometry and materials, released when no layer uses them), so 20 layers of any shape stay smooth. A wireframe cage, a glass box and a capsule are left out as shapes of their own: the _Wireframe_ and _Glow_ options give any shape the first two looks, and a capsule sits between the cylinder and the ellipsoid.

- **Label styles, label colours, chosen labels and layer label styles.** **Halo is the default**: every node label now has a thin rim against its colour (white around dark text, dark around light text), so it reads over nodes, edges and any theme — in new networks, every theme and older sessions alike (they never saved a label look, so nothing in the session format argues for plain); _Plain_ stays one click away. Edit → Style has more node rows. _Label style_: _Halo (outline)_ (a rim around the letters, adjustable width), _Box_ (a rounded box behind them, adjustable opacity), _Shadow_ or _Plain_, each optionally _Bold_; the rim, box or shadow colour is picked against the text (white around dark text on light themes, dark around light text on dark themes) unless you choose one. _Label position_: right of the node (as before), centred on it, above or below — centred with a bold halo gives labels that sit on their nodes and read over any fill or edge. _Colour labels by_ colours the labels of the target nodes apart from the nodes: one palette colour per layer, cluster or first annotation term (the theme's node or channel colours, Distinct, Okabe–Ito or the new _Tol bright_, made to read on the background), the node's own colour, or a colour that reads on the node (_Contrast with node_). Edit → Attributes has _Label colour_ and _Label style_ columns and a _Label colours_ control for the ticked rows (or the list); the inspector has the label style (colour, halo width or box opacity, bold) and position. All of it applies to the selected nodes, a layer or all, is one undo step each, is kept in sessions (`label_style`, `label_position`, written only when set; older sessions load as before) and in node attribute files (`LabelStyle`, e.g. `halo #ffffff 3 bold`, and `LabelPosition`), and shows in PNG exports, which now also draw the label style's halo and background. _Tol bright_ joins the node and channel palettes too. **Chosen labels** stay on screen whatever is selected: Edit → Nodes → _Show Labels_ has a fourth choice, _Chosen_ (with _and the selection's labels_ to add the selected nodes'), _Show Labels of Selected Nodes_ (after Find, a group or any selection — no mouse needed) and _Clear Chosen Labels_; a _Label shown_ row in Edit → Style (a layer, the selection or all), a _Label shown_ column in Edit → Attributes (every ticked row at once), a box in the inspector and right-click → Selected nodes → _Show Their Labels_ / _Hide Their Labels_ choose them too. Choosing labels while Show Labels is on Selected or None switches it to Chosen. Undoable; sessions keep the chosen labels and the Show Labels choice (`label_shown`, `nodeLabels`; older sessions load with the default), and node attribute files carry `LabelShown`. **Labels to show** (Edit → Nodes): every node with a tick box, filtered by text, layer or group, with _All_ / _None_ / _Invert_ / _The selected nodes_ on the listed rows and a count; Enter in the filter ticks every match, Tab and Space tick one; only the rows in view are drawn. Edit → Attributes gains _Label size_, _Bold_ and _Label position_ columns (with _Label_, _Label colour_, _Label style_ and _Label shown_, each sortable and changing all ticked rows at once; bold and a style keep each node's own look), node attribute files carry `Label`, `LabelColor`, `LabelSize`, `LabelStyle`, `LabelPosition`, `LabelBold` and `LabelShown`, and `POST /api/attributes/nodes` refuses an unreadable label value with a message naming the column. **Layer labels** (Edit → Layers → _Labels & axes_ → _Label style_): halo by default, plain, box or shadow with their colour and width or opacity; font (as the app, sans-serif, serif, monospace, condensed), bold, italic; one colour, each layer's own colour or a palette; at the floor's outer corner, centred above or under the floor — for all or the selected layers, undoable, in sessions (`label_style`, `label_font`, `label_bold`, `label_italic`, `label_color`, `label_position` on layers, only when set) and in image exports; applying a theme's colours gives them the theme's colour again, keeping colours keeps theirs.
- **Interactive circular views: Gosling.js, NGCircos.js, CircosJS and JBrowse 2.** Export → Visualizations has four new folded sub-sections, alternatives to Circos that run in a browser. Each takes the layers ticked in Layer selection (all if none) and the channels ticked for layouts and shown in the scene, and draws the layers as segments of a ring sized by their node counts, the nodes in order along them and the edges between them, in the scene's colours. _Gosling.js_ downloads a circular spec (layers as a custom assembly, a node ring, degree bars, edges as arcs within a layer and links between layers, coloured by channel) or opens it in the online Gosling editor, the node and edge tables hosted on this server for one hour. _NGCircos.js_ and _CircosJS_ download a page that loads the library from a CDN at a fixed version, with a histogram or heatmap ring of node degree or of a File → Values column, and the data as JSON. _JBrowse 2_ stands the network in for a genome — each layer a sequence, each edge a VCF breakend that the Circular View draws as a chord — and opens it in JBrowse Web or downloads the config, VCF and FASTA for JBrowse Web or Desktop. Names are escaped wherever a tool shows them as HTML; above 3,000 edges the status line warns that the view may be slow, and 20,000 is the limit. Arena3D gains no new dependency: it writes JSON and HTML text only.

Sessions keep the spread positions.
- **View → Annotations highlights groups in the scene.** A new panel in the View menu, after Scene & Camera, gathers what was spread over Analyze → Annotations & Groups, in five cards with a one-line hint after each title (the first open, Expand all / Collapse all at the top). _What to show_: draw around nothing, the clusters or the annotations as one segmented choice (the group list follows it), the annotation set with _Rename_ and _Delete_, a line summing it up (for example _GO BP: 42 groups over 310 nodes_) and links to where annotations come from. _How to draw_: _Draw as_, seven shapes as tiles with a sketch of each, drawn round every group on each layer in the group's colour — on the floor a _Hull_ or an _Outline_ (a band along the hull's edge); soft, a _Fog cloud_ (the NORMA haze over the members) or a _Cloud_ (a round cloud of puffs); or a 3D container, a _Sphere_, an _Ellipsoid_ fitted to the group's extent, or a _Box_, translucent with their outline drawn — and new _Opacity_ and _Padding_ sliders for every shape. _On the nodes_: pie charts, and _Colour Nodes by Group_ / _Colour Labels by Group_, which give nodes or labels their first group's colour in one undo step. _Groups_: the list under a header row, with swatch, name, size, a _Show_ switch and _select_ in aligned columns, the count of groups, the filter and _Show: All_ / _None_. _Legend & reports_: the legend, Groups Table, Overlap Table and Group Stats, with _Collapse into Super Nodes_ set apart. Analyze → Annotations & Groups keeps the STRING enrichment and an _Open Annotations…_ button, the Networks menu's _Annotation options…_ opens the panel, and the Clustering panel's _Draw Around Clusters…_ opens it drawing round the clusters. Every control keeps its id. Help has a View → Annotations page.
- **Sessions keep how annotation groups are drawn.** A saved session now records View → Annotations' settings — _Draw around_ (nothing, clusters or annotations), _Draw as_ (any of the seven shapes), _Opacity_, _Padding_ and _Show Annotations as Pie Charts_ — and the groups whose shape is switched off in the Groups card, in one optional `annotationView` block, and puts them back on load (also when switching between loaded networks). Sessions saved before this load as they always did.
- **Plots (Analyze → Plots).** Distributions of one measure — any node metric of the Metrics panel (degree, betweenness, closeness, clustering coefficient, eigenvector, PageRank, coreness, eccentricity, multiplex degree, participation coefficient, multilayer PageRank), the edge weights per channel, a node value column (File → Values), the component sizes or the shortest-path lengths — as a histogram (bin count or width), a kernel density, a CDF or CCDF, a log–log distribution with an optional power-law fit (maximum likelihood above k_min, after Clauset et al. 2009, flagged as a simple fit) or box and violin plots per layer; and plots that relate topological features: a scatter of one metric against another with Pearson and Spearman correlations, a Spearman correlation matrix of all metrics, C(k) and knn(k) against degree, the rich-club coefficient (normalised against degree-preserving rewired graphs), k-core shells, a centrality ranking with each node's rank in every layer, and layer profiles over the global statistics. Each layer overlaid in its colour with a legend, or the analysed layers as one graph; summary statistics (n, mean, median, sd, min, max) under the chart; SVG, PNG and CSV downloads; a click on a bar, box or point (or a box dragged on a scatter) selects its nodes. New endpoint `POST /api/plots` (worker pool) returns the metrics and curves, which the panel keeps per network so each is computed once. The robustness curves now use the same chart component.
- **Network perturbation analysis (Analyze → Perturbation).** _Knock out_ nodes — the selected ones, names (every copy in the analysed layers), or the top _k_ by degree, betweenness or PageRank (in the whole network or in each layer), optionally every copy across layers (a multiplex knockout) — and edges: the selected edges, a channel, the inter-layer edges or a seeded random share. Data Tables → _Knockout_ compares each analysed layer and the whole network before and after (nodes, edges, components, largest component, density, average path length, diameter, global efficiency, nodes cut off from the largest component); _Knockout Nodes_ lists each node's degree, betweenness and harmonic closeness before and after, most affected first, with the nodes knocked out or cut off. The scene fades or hides the knocked-out nodes and edges, _Colour by Change_ colours nodes on a diverging scale (blue lost, red gained) with a legend, _Restore_ puts the looks back, all undoable, and _Apply as a New Network_ loads the perturbed network through the Import as popup and the Networks menu; right-click → _Selected nodes_ → _Knock Out the N Nodes…_ runs it on a selection. Optional network propagation (random walk with restart from seed nodes) before and after the knockout. _Attack and robustness_ removes nodes step by step — random failure averaged over several runs, or targeted by degree or betweenness, ranked once or recalculated after each step — and plots the relative size of the largest component (plus the number of components and, up to 2,000 nodes, the global efficiency) per layer and for the whole network, with the robustness R (Schneider et al. 2011); strategies can share the chart, which downloads as SVG and CSV and fills Data Tables → _Robustness_. Progress bars with Cancel: an attack is computed one curve per request (the whole network first), so its bar counts the curves and Cancel keeps the ones done; a knockout shows its seconds; a bar ends green with a summary or red with the error. New endpoints `POST /api/perturbation/knockout` and `/api/perturbation/attack` run in the worker pool; curves have at most 50 steps and 20 random runs, a request stops after 30 s (`ARENA_PERTURBATION_SECONDS`) with what it has, and above 2,000 nodes betweenness and closeness are estimated from 200 sampled nodes.
- **Cosmograph export.** Export → Other tools → _Send to Cosmograph_: a links CSV (`source`, `target`, `weight`, `channel`, `source_layer`, `target_layer`; nodes named node_layer, unique across layers) and a nodes CSV (`id`, `label`, `layer`, `color`, `size`, `cluster`, `annotations`, the File → Values columns, the node metrics applied so far and the UMAP view's `umap_x`/`umap_y` when computed). _Open in Cosmograph_ hosts both for one hour through `POST /api/share` (downloads in a sandbox, with CORS) and opens `cosmograph.app/run/?data=…&meta=…`, coloured, sized and labelled by those columns.
- **UMAP layouts and a UMAP view.** _UMAP_ (McInnes, Healy & Melville 2018, umap-learn on the server) is the last algorithm of the Layout panel, in 2D or 3D, on every scope, with three modes: _Topology UMAP_ places nodes by their connections (one- and two-step random-walk neighbourhoods, weighted with _Use Edge Weights_), _Attribute UMAP_ by the features ticked under it (File → Values columns, annotation sets and clusters as 0/1 terms, node metrics computed on the server; standardised, missing values take the column mean), and _Hybrid UMAP_ by both, with a balance slider (0 = topology, 1 = attributes). Neighbours (n_neighbors, 15), minimum distance (min_dist, 0.1), Euclidean or cosine distance and a fixed random seed (42) make runs repeatable; _Align Nodes Across Selected Layers_ gives a node's copies one spot. ## [3.1.0] - 2026-08-10

### Added

- Network upload accepts a minimal 2-column edgelist (`SourceNode`, `TargetNode` only) — all nodes are spread out in a single default layer named `Layer1`. The optional `Weight` and `Channel` columns still apply. A downloadable example lives in the Help → Examples tab.

- Edge weight can now be shown as **edge thickness**, not only opacity. The Edge Actions panel replaces the "Edge Opacity By Weight" checkbox with a "Show Edge Weight As" radio — Nothing / Opacity / Width / Both — plus intra- and inter-layer width sliders for whichever property weight isn't driving. Sessions carry the choice as the independent `edgeOpacityByWeight` and `edgeWidthByWeight` booleans; files written before this default to opacity, so they render unchanged. Thickness needed `Line2` (instanced quads) because WebGL renders every line primitive at exactly 1px regardless of `linewidth`.

### Changed

- Channel curvature sliders reach much further — intra-layer 10–60 (was 10–20), inter-layer 1–30 (was 1–10) — so the channels of a multi-channel edge can be pulled well apart instead of running nearly parallel. Defaults are unchanged, so existing sessions render exactly as before.

### Fixed

- Edges no longer glow. Thick edges cover a large share of the screen, and bloom on all of them was blinding against dark backgrounds. Bloom is now selective (three's off-screen bloom-composer pattern) and reads from node spheres alone, so nodes still glow, edges and layer planes stay crisp, and the extra pass costs a few spheres rather than a second full scene.
- Curved channel edges rendered as dotted lines with beads at the curve points once thickness landed. `LineMaterial`'s `worldUnits` mode assumes a perspective camera — its fragment shader traces a view ray from the camera origin and discards anything farther than half a width from the segment, which under this app's orthographic camera discards along the whole segment. Widths are now screen-space, sized against a shared resolution uniform kept on the frustum size (so the numbers still mean world units), retargeted on resize and for the PNG export.
- Node colors rendered washed out/dark in the 3D scene compared to the 2D navigator. The bloom composer was blitting linear color straight to the sRGB canvas (missing `OutputPass`), and the ambient light was left at the pre-r155 intensity that physical lighting divides by PI.

## [3.0.0] - 2026-07-27

Full rewrite: migrated from R/Shiny to a **FastAPI (Python) backend + Vite / TypeScript / Three.js frontend**. Algorithms (layouts, clustering, topology) ported 1:1 via python-igraph.

### Added

- **Satellite view** — minimap overview of the scene (hidden layers excluded).
- **Command-pattern undo/redo** — granular history for scene mutations.
- **Token-shared sessions** — hand off a session to another app via `?session=` URL (`POST /api/external`).
- **Export Image** — button to export the scene as PNG.
- Reproducible node scatter (seeded), 60 FPS render loop as default, redesigned UI (side-drawer panels).

### Changed

- **Zoom to cursor** — mouse-wheel zoom anchors at the pointer instead of the scene center.

### Fixed

- **Undraggable layers when zoomed in** — orthographic drag ray rejected planes sitting behind its origin; layers froze at high zoom despite hover working.
