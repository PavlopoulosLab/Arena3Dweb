# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed

- A Shift + Drag that started on a floor moved that layer by the first mouse step before selecting (the layer drag saw the move first); Shift + Drag now never grabs a floor.
- A hive plot hid the scene's own axes only if the Show Scene Coord System box was ticked, but a newly loaded network draws them whatever the box says, so a second hive plot in a row left them crossing its centre; it now goes by what is on screen.
- In a flat hive plot, links between opposite axes (e.g. up and down with four axes) ran straight through the centre, on top of the axes; they now curve round the centre.
- Arranging layers as a chord diagram fitted the view as an undo step of its own, recorded when the camera animation ended, so one Undo took back either the view or the arrangement depending on timing; the fit is now part of the arrangement's step (as for the hive plot), and the e2e test that passed only by that timing checks the real result.
- Navigation Controls ignored quick clicks (the first step waited 70 ms, so a click released sooner did nothing) and a held button stopped at the slightest mouse movement, or kept going when released outside it. Every button now steps on press; held down it repeats until released anywhere, through small movements, after a 300 ms pause, so a click stays one step. The spread/pull buttons repeat too, each press is one undo step, the buttons never select text, and they work with touch and pen.
- OmniPath fetched nothing for gene names typed in lower case, for UniProt IDs and for a single protein, and crashed (HTTP 500) for organisms OmniPath does not cover; names are sent as OmniPath expects them (TP53 for human, Trp53 for mouse and rat), UniProt IDs match, and other organisms get a clear message.
- IntAct crashed (HTTP 500) whenever interaction partners were included — names such as `"ccsb isoform id: ndn_2"` broke the import's table — and could not match UniProt IDs among the proteins; quotes are dropped from imported names (all importers), and IntAct matches gene names or any identifier.
- When nothing is found among the typed proteins, IntAct and OmniPath now say how many interactions with other partners exist and which checkbox includes them.
- The report tabs inside Data Tables were laid out as drawers of their own (the drawer rules matched every `.tab-pane`), which cut the tables on wide drawers; the rules now apply to the top-level panels only.

### Security

- Quadratic layouts (Kamada-Kawai, MDS, Graphopt, Sugiyama) are limited to 2,000 nodes per layout (`ARENA_MAX_QUADRATIC_NODES`), and layouts, clustering, metrics and statistics run off the event loop, so one request cannot stall or exhaust the service.
- Shared exports (`GET /api/share/{token}`) are served as downloads in a sandbox (`Content-Disposition: attachment`, a `sandbox` content-security policy, `nosniff`), never as pages of the application's origin.
- Links answered by a NORMA server and the Flame address must be web addresses (`http(s)`); node links passed to `POST /api/open` must be `http(s)` or `mailto` links.
- Database imports have a time budget for the whole request (`ARENA_DB_TOTAL_SECONDS`, 150 s by default).

### Added

- **Redraw button.** Next to Navigation Controls, _Redraw_ (↻) puts the scene right when something gets stuck: it lets go of a drag, a held axis key or an area outline left half done (a release outside the window), drops a grabbed floor, hides a loading veil left up by a failed request, recomputes active edge bundles and redraws nodes, edges and labels at the window's size, keeping the view, the selection and undo. A frame step that throws no longer freezes the picture: the error is logged once and the frame is drawn.
- **20 layers and 10 edge channels.** A network may now hold up to 20 layers (was 18) and 10 edge channels (was 9) — uploads, sessions, database imports, layer splits and merges, and the same-name link channel all follow; `/api/config` reports `max_layers: 20` and `max_channels: 10`, with 20 layer colours and 10 channel colours for light and dark themes (Set1 plus a cyan). The Home page and Help give the new limits.
- **Freehand lasso and area modes.** Shift + Drag selects a rectangle or, chosen under _Area Shape_ in the Navigation Controls, a freehand lasso (the nodes inside the drawn outline, concave shapes included; on big networks the lasso is tested on release so drawing stays smooth). _Area Mode_ makes the area replace the selection (_New_), add to it (_Add_, as before) or take its nodes out (_Remove_; Alt / Option held at release removes too). Both choices are remembered in the browser.
- **More ways to select (Analyze → Explore).** _Select All_, _Invert_ and _Clear_ (nodes and edges); _Connected Component_ (every node the selection reaches, within or across layers); _Nodes of Selected Layers_; _Nodes with at least N links_ (hubs, within layers or counting inter-layer links).
- **Highlight paths between selected nodes.** A switch in the Explore card draws the shortest paths joining every pair of selected nodes (up to 25), within or across layers, in the edge selection colour, and follows the selection as it changes; _Also select the nodes along the paths_ selects the nodes in between too. Switched off, it takes back only what it added. A `selection:changed` bus event announces every change to the node selection.
- **Hive plots in 3D.** Two options in the Hive plot arrangement. _Axes_: in a plane (classic) or _spread in 3D_ — a right-angled tripod for three layers, a tetrahedron for four (every two axes 109.5° apart), ±x ±y ±z for six, otherwise evenly over a sphere — so every two axes are neighbours and no link crosses an axis (the paper keeps to three axes because, in a plane, links between non-neighbouring axes do). _Height off the plane_ (axes in a plane): a second property (degree, betweenness, clustering coefficient or name) lifts each node off the plane, putting two hive-panel views in one plot; the view tilts to show the depth. Links curve in the plane of their two axes and carry the height along; sessions keep the choice (`hive.axes`). Three more examples open as 3D hive plots: GPCR signalling (a tetrahedron), the SARS-CoV-2 host–virus network (five axes on a sphere) and six layers along ±x ±y ±z.
- **Hive plot layout (multilayered mode).** Layout → Layer Arrangement → _Hive plot (layers as axes)_, after Krzywinski M, Birol I, Jones S, Marra MA (2012), Hive plots — rational approach to visualizing networks, _Briefings in Bioinformatics_ 13(5):627–644 (doi:10.1093/bib/bbr069): every layer becomes an axis radiating from one centre (its floor a thin bar, its name at the outer end), and every node sits on its axis by _degree_, _betweenness centrality_, _clustering coefficient_ or _name_, on a scale of each axis full length (the paper's "absolute connectivity with normalized axis length"), one scale for all axes, or rank. Links curve between the axes; links within one axis arc beside it, where the paper draws them to the axis's clone. The view faces you and the scene axes step aside; undo, any other arrangement and saved sessions (`hive`, floor shape `axis`) all handle it. Three examples open as hive plots: PREGO (three axes), GPCR signalling (four) and the SARS-CoV-2 host–virus network (five).
- **Fruchterman-Reingold on every new network.** The Import as popup has _Lay out with Fruchterman-Reingold_, ticked: a new network is laid out layer by layer at once (only the new layers when adding), instead of keeping random positions; a single 3D network is laid out in 3D as before, and an expanded view keeps its places.
- **Same-name links, on request.** The Import as popup has _Link nodes with the same name across layers_: ticked, every node's copies in consecutive layers are joined (missing links added); unticked, a database import's own links are left out and added layers are not linked. Ticked by default for database imports, unticked for files.
- **Progress bars in the Database Importers.** Each card has its own bar under its button, following every step — fetching (with elapsed seconds; each STRING call when the browser asks STRING directly), building the scene, laying out — and turning green when done.
- **Sort search hits.** The Reactome and Gene Ontology search hits sort by name (default), ID or as found.
- **NDEx examples.** Three public networks load with one click: NCI-PID p53 pathway, SIGNOR Inhibition of Apoptosis and the SARS-CoV-2/Human interactome.
- **Expand a single 3D network in place.** _Single 3D network_ + _Add to the current network_: the new nodes and edges join the graph itself (a node already there by name is that node, no copies or links), what was there keeps its place, colour, size, description and channel colours, new nodes are placed next to the nodes they link to, and the view stays a single 3D network — so fetching more STRING interactors grows the network in place. From a multilayered view the two networks become one graph, laid out in 3D; _Multilayered_ + _Add_ from a single 3D view spreads it back onto floors first.
- **Parallel channel edges, as in STRING.** Edit → Edges → _Edge Shape_ (multi-channel networks): _Parallel_ — every channel its own straight line, side by side across the pair (also along bundled paths) — is the new default; _Bézier curves_ (the former look, with the curvature sliders) and _Straight_ (the channels share one line) are the alternatives. Saved in sessions (`edgeShape`).
- **Import as, in a popup, on every load.** Every load — File → Upload Network, an example, any Database Importers card, a saved session or a shared `?session=` link — first asks two things: _Show as_ a **Multilayered network** (every layer on its own floor) or a **Single 3D network** (one graph, no floors), and _Into the view_: **Replace the current network** or **Add to the current network** (new layers with copies linked, when multilayered; the graph expanded in place, when single 3D; greyed out until a network is loaded). A saved session or shared link always replaces; only the view is asked. The popup comes before anything is uploaded or fetched, so _Cancel_ (or Esc) loads nothing, and it offers the previous answers again. For one-layer networks it replaces the separate one-layer prompt. The radio group at the top of the Database Importers panel is gone.
- **STRING importer with NORMA's options.** Database Importers → STRING now offers everything NORMA's STRING card does: _Proteins or genes_ (Ctrl+Enter fetches; names are looked up in STRING first and unmatched ones are reported), 19 organisms plus any NCBI taxon ID, _Network_ (functional associations or physical interactions only), _Interactors to add_ (0–500, default 20), _Minimum confidence_ presets (0.900 / 0.700 / 0.400 / 0.150 / custom), _Evidence channels_ in STRING's colours with the confidence recomputed from the ticked channels only (STRING's prior-corrected rule, pairs below the minimum dropped), _Edges_ as one per channel or one per pair (combined score), _Groupings from functional terms_ (enriched terms with an FDR cut-off or all annotations; 21 term collections plus any other STRING offers, with All/None, groups per collection and smallest group) — each collection becomes an annotation set named after the query (e.g. _STRING TP53, MDM2: KEGG pathways_), so later imports never overwrite it — and a _STRING server_ section: connect through this server, directly from the browser or automatically (the browser takes over when the server cannot reach STRING), a version address (e.g. https://version-12-0.string-db.org) for reproducible answers, and protein descriptions on the nodes. _Fetch groupings for the current view_ adds STRING groupings to any loaded network. `POST /api/import/string` takes `network_type`, `edge_mode`, `descriptions`, `groupings`, `address` and `raw` (STRING's answers fetched by the browser) and returns `string_version`, `taxon_name`, `node_info` and `groupings` besides the network; new `POST /api/import/string/groupings`.
- **Edge bundling in Single network mode.** One _Edge Bundling_ slider in Edit → Edges bundles every edge of the graph as one set, the former inter-layer edges included, as in NORMA; back in Multilayered mode the intra- and inter-layer sliders return with their strengths, and a mode switch rebuilds the bundles.
- **Node metrics on an undirected, directed or multi-edged graph.** Analyze → Node Metrics → _Treat the network as_: parallel edges merged (as before), source → target direction kept, or every channel its own edge (`graph` in `POST /api/topology`).
- **Layer selection tab.** The layers to work on are ticked in a tab of their own in the top bar (Multilayered mode) — the floor colour and node count per row, Hide and Labels switches, Select/Deselect All, Invert and a count; the Layout panel's first section reports the count and opens the tab, and Analysis, NORMA and Flame follow the same ticks.
- **Sortable reports.** Every Data Tables report sorts by a click on a column title (numbers largest first, text A→Z; a second click turns the order round); the order survives a refresh and the CSV follows it.
- **Data Tables redrawn.** A tab strip that scrolls sideways, reports as proper tables — bordered cells, a header that stays put, numbers right-aligned in tabular figures, a toolbar with the row count and the CSV.
- **UpSet plots.** Compare Layers, the group Overlap Table and Compare Networks add an UpSet plot of the sets they compare (nodes per layer, members per group, nodes per layer of both networks), with an SVG download.
- **Chord diagram arrangement.** Layer Arrangement → _Chord diagram_: the floors lie flat on one ring facing the viewer, each a sector with its nodes strung along the arc, so intra-layer edges hug the ring and inter-layer edges cross it as chords; the nodes move with the floors and undo brings both back.
- **Sankey exports.** Export → Visualizations: a Sankey diagram (SVG) with the layers as columns and the inter-layer edges as flows, per node, per group or per layer, plus the same flows as SankeyMATIC text and a Plotly figure (JSON).
- **Send to Flame.** Export → Other tools: the node names of the ticked layers go to Flame for functional enrichment through its `url_genes` link API, one gene list per layer or the selected layers combined, or copied as text to paste.
- **Export panel as cards; section titles everywhere.** The Export panel's five sections fold and unfold like the Databases cards; the Nodes, Edges, Layers, Scene and File panels got section titles with a rule above each, so their controls read as groups.
- **Layout panel per network mode, the NORMA way in Single network mode.** In _Single network_ mode the panel is the layout of one graph: the layer section, scope and alignment step aside, the _Groups (NORMA-2.0)_ card opens by itself with the clusters or annotation terms as groups, a _Draw groups as_ choice (fog clouds or hulls, as NORMA draws its groups; fog clouds after a group layout unless chosen otherwise) and a pie-chart switch. _Multilayered_ mode keeps the layer-centric controls.
- **No impossible layout combinations.** 3D Layout and Clustering disable each other, the local layout is only enabled with a clustering or the Selected Nodes Only scope, Edge Weights only with the weighted algorithms on the plain scopes without clustering or alignment, and Cross-Layer Alignment only with Per Layer, 2D and no clustering — each with a note saying why (the help page lists what goes together).
- **Layer Arrangement in the Layout panel, three new arrangements.** A card with Parallel Coordinates, Zig Zag, Star, Cube, Helix, Sphere, Grid, the new _Circle_ (floors on a ring facing the centre), _Stack_ (horizontal shelves) and _Fan_ (pages around a spine), and By Inter-Layer Links — _Arrange Layers_ applies it, undo restores; Scene & Camera offers the same list.
- **Cross-Layer Alignment has its own run.** _Run Aligned Layout_ sets the Per Layer scope, a 2D layout and no clustering and runs the chosen algorithm with the alignment; picking a mode now says that a run is needed; the misalignment score shows the effect.
- **Bolder layer coordinate systems.** Each layer's axes are sized by its floor, with thick shafts, big letters and an origin marker.
- **Several loaded networks.** File → _Loaded networks_ lists every network loaded from a file, example, database or link; switching restores each one as it was (layout, colours, shapes, annotations, mode), and _Compare Networks_ reports layers, nodes, node copies, edges and per-layer nodes in both / one only with Jaccard indices (Data Tables → Network Comparison).
- **Annotation sets.** Each attribute or group file, enrichment or cluster run is kept as a named set in Analyze → Annotations; switch between them or to none.
- **Group statistics, NORMA-style.** Groups → _Group Stats_: members, layers, edges inside (intra/inter), density, mean degree, share of edges and overlap with other groups per group; and every table button now opens the Data Tables panel on its tab instead of filling it unseen.
- **Label style.** Edit → Nodes → _Label Style_: colour, font, bold, italic, dark halo, background and opacity for all node labels or for the selected layers; undoable.
- **Clustering as its own section.** Layout → _3 · Clustering_ finds communities without moving the nodes (`POST /api/clustering`: within each layer, over the selected layers as one graph, or shared across layers by node name), colours them, lists them in Data Tables, and _Use Clusters as Annotations_ turns them into annotation terms for pies, hulls, fog clouds and groups; undoable.
- **Annotation options follow the data.** Pie charts, annotation hulls/fog clouds, annotations as groups (Layout and Analyze) and annotation colouring are disabled, with a note, while the network has no annotations; they switch on when an attribute or group file, a session or an enrichment brings some.
- **One-layer networks ask how to show up.** Loading a network with a single layer shows a prompt: _Single 3D network_ (Single network mode, laid out in 3D at once) or _Single layer on a floor_ (Multilayered mode); sessions keep their saved state.
- **Unlimited zoom and a fit-to-view double-click.** The wheel and pinch zoom no longer stop at 0.2–2×; a double-click on the background (and _Recenter Network_) fits the whole network — every visible floor and node — back into the window, as one undo step.
- **Axes drawn as coordinate systems.** The scene axes and the layer axes are arrows with heads, dashed negative halves, tick marks and X/Y/Z letters, with an opacity slider for each (Scene & Camera, Edit → Layers).
- **Export tab.** Every export moved out of File into its own top-bar panel: Image & 3D (PNG at 2048–8192 px, SVG, GLB, rotation video), **Cytoscape** (_Send to Cytoscape_ opens the network in a running Cytoscape desktop through cyREST with layers side by side and node colour, size, shape, layer, edge channel and weight as table columns; CX2, Cytoscape.js JSON and SIF + attribute tables as files), **Visualizations** (a chord/Circos-style diagram and an adjacency heatmap as SVG, the matrix as CSV, Circos input files; one-click hand-offs: an interactive heatmap in the Vega editor with the chart in the link, the network in Gephi Lite through a GraphML hosted for an hour by the new `POST /api/share` · `GET /api/share/{token}`, and the selected proteins on the STRING website), GraphML, MuxViz, NORMA and the session.
- **Find tab.** Node and edge search in their own panel: exact, contains, starts with, ends with or regular expression, case-sensitive or not, with camera focus. Edge search matches either end, the source, the target, both ends or the channel; matches are selected (Data Tables → Selected Edges) and listed, each focusing on its two nodes.
- **Databases → Import as.** A fetched network can replace the loaded one, join it as new layer(s) — every layer and node stays where it was, the new layers line up after the last one, and every copy of a node is linked to the copies with the same name in the other layers — or load as a single 3D network.
- **Floor styles.** Edit → Layers → _Floor Style_: solid, wireframe, grid, dotted grid or outline only (over a faint plane), for the selected layers or all; saved in sessions (`floor_style`), undoable. Replaces _Show Floors in Wireframes_.
- **Fourteen node shapes, per layer.** Capsule, torus, icosahedron, dodecahedron, pyramid, disc, hexagonal prism and ring join sphere, cube, diamond, cone, cylinder and tetrahedron; Edit → Nodes applies a shape to all nodes or to the selected layers, so each layer can have its own; Colour-Blind Mode uses them all.
- **Theme-aware container colours.** Every theme carries a container colour that contrasts with its background; cubes, spheres and fog clouds without a colour of their own follow it (_Theme default_ in 3D Container Look) and recolour when the theme changes.
- **Single-network mode mouse.** With no layers to drag or turn, the left button orbits the network, the middle button pans, Z/X/C + drag turn it about one axis, floors are never hover-targets, and the navigation table lists the controls of the current mode.
- **Coloured menus.** Each top-bar menu has its own hue — a tint when it is open or active — so the ten entries read apart at a glance.
- **Performance panel.** View → Performance offers, besides the frame rate, the render resolution (pixel ratio), node detail (sphere mesh), hiding labels and inter-layer edges while moving, the navigator refresh rate, three presets (Quality, Balanced, Fast) and a live readout of frames per second, draw calls, triangles, lines, counts, memory and pixel ratio.
- **Help with screenshots and readable formats.** Every page opens with a screenshot of its panel (`npm run help:shots` refreshes them); the API page explains the `/api/open` and session JSON with field tables and highlighted examples; new Find and Export pages; the layout progress bar names the clustering algorithm too.
- **Databases panel as NORMA-style cards, with Reactome and Gene Ontology.** Every source is an expandable, colour-coded card with its own fields, Import button and a _?_ to the help page: STRING, Reactome, OmniPath, NDEx, IntAct, Gene Ontology and Compare databases. Proteins, organism and score typed in one card reach the others. _Reactome_ and _Gene Ontology_ import pathways or GO terms as layers — search by name or paste identifiers; each set becomes a layer of its genes with STRING's interactions among them, copies linked across layers (`POST /api/import/reactome`, `/api/import/go`, `GET /api/import/reactome/search`, `/api/import/go/search`). A wrong identifier is now a 400 with a message rather than a 502.
- **Progress bars for layouts and analyses.** Running a layout, a group layout or a node metric shows a bar in the panel: the share of the network sent, then a striped "computing on the server" bar with the seconds elapsed, then "applied".
- **3D layouts list only 3D-capable algorithms.** With _3D Layout_ on, algorithms without a 3D version are hidden (a non-3D choice falls back to Fruchterman-Reingold); stacking a multilayer network into Single-network mode switches 3D Layout on.
- **Help: How-to page and readable file formats.** A new _How to…_ page with step-by-step recipes (load a network, build one from databases, align layers, show groups, colour by expression, find paths, untangle, single-network view, figures, sharing, phones, undo). File-format examples are now shown as tables with a caption instead of tab-separated text whose columns did not line up.
- **More ideas from NORMA.** A _Groups_ card (Analyze) lists every annotation term or cluster with its colour (editable — pies and hulls follow), member counts per layer, a per-group hull switch and a select button, plus Groups and Overlap (Jaccard) tables, a _Legend_ button and _Collapse into Super Nodes_ (one node per group and layer, sized by members, member edges summed; undo restores). File → _Upload GROUP file_ reads NORMA's annotation format (`Group<TAB>member, member, …` or Node/Group rows); _Upload node VALUES_ reads a numeric table (expression, scores) and Edit → Style colours nodes by any of its columns with a diverging scale when values cross zero. An on-canvas **legend** (Scene & Camera → Show Legend) shows the layers after a load and whatever was coloured last — layers, groups, a metric or value gradient — and clicking an entry selects its members. New endpoints `POST /api/attributes/groups` and `POST /api/attributes/values`; new Data Tables: Groups, Group Overlap, Node Values.
- **Network mode switch.** The top bar chooses _Multilayered_ (layers as floors, every layer control) or _Single network_ (one graph: several layers are stacked into one 3D space and laid out at once, a single layer loses its floor, every edge follows the same settings, and the layer-only controls — layer selection, cross-layer alignment, layer arrangements, the Layers panel, inter-layer sliders, layer comparison — step aside). A one-layer network opens in Single-network mode; sessions remember the mode; undo keeps the switch truthful. It replaces the _3D Network (ignore layers)_ checkbox.
- **API for other tools.** `POST /api/open` opens a network in Arena3D from a list of edges (or Arena3D TSV text) with optional per-node colour, size, url, description and annotations: the server parses it like an upload, lays every layer out (any Layout-panel algorithm, or random), stores a session for one hour and returns the link. Interactive API documentation now lives at `/api/docs` (`/api/redoc`, `/api/openapi.json`), reachable through the production proxy, with a summary, tag and description on every endpoint. Cross-origin requests are allowed. A shared link that has expired is now reported in the File panel instead of a silent empty scene. Help → API and the README describe the endpoints with curl and Python examples.
- **Databases panel.** The database importers moved out of the File panel into their own top-menu panel, one card per importer as in NORMA — STRING (evidence channels), STRING · IntAct · OmniPath (one layer each) and NDEx — each with its own Import button; the query proteins, organism and score are shared.
- **Edge bundling.** Edit → Edges has Intra-Layer and Inter-Layer bundling sliders (0–1) that merge similar edges into shared bundles with force-directed edge bundling (Holten & van Wijk 2009, in 3D, neighbours from a midpoint grid). Paths are computed once and cached in a camera-independent frame (layer-local / scene-local), so rotating costs nothing; the sliders blend straight and bundled; bundles stretch with moving nodes and are recomputed once the scene settles. Channels run side by side along the bundle. Strengths are saved in sessions (`intraEdgeBundling`, `interEdgeBundling`) and each change is an undo step.
- **Friendlier Layout panel.** Numbered sections in the order of work (1 · Layers, 2 · Layout), plain-language scope choices, switches for weights and 3D, clustering and its local layout side by side, a _Run Layout_ bar that stays in view with the status and misalignment score, and Cross-Layer Alignment and Group Layout folded below. Clustering now uses the chosen local layout inside clusters (the main algorithm when none is chosen).
- **3D container look.** Edit → Layers → 3D Container Look sets the colour (or the floor colour) and opacity of cube, sphere and fog-cloud containers; saved in sessions (`container_color`, `container_opacity`), undoable.
- **More scene themes.** Midnight, Blueprint, Solarized, Forest, Paper, Pastel and High Contrast join Dark, Light and Gray. The theme picker moved from the canvas into the top bar, so it is no longer hidden under an open panel.
- **Load Example on the Home page.** _Try an example_ picks and loads any of the 13 bundled examples (saved sessions where they exist; the added SARS-CoV-2 3D network without arrows); the _Download examples_ card is gone (the files stay in Help → Examples).
- **Fog clouds.** Analyze → Annotations & Enrichment → Hulls Around can now draw each cluster or annotation group as a NORMA-style fog cloud (soft, tinted haze following the group's members, also in 3D layouts) instead of a hull. The _Cloud_ layer shape becomes a _Fog cloud_ container in the same style: soft puffs of fog filling the layer's volume around its 3D layout.
- **New examples and an example chooser.** File → Example picks what _Load Example_ opens: four weighted layers, a network without layers (SARS-CoV-2 2-column edgelist), a 3D network with layers ignored (SARS-CoV-2 session), layers as spheres (GPCR session) or the directed three-channel aspirin network.
- **Sessions keep the 3D state.** Saved sessions record hidden floors (`floor_visible`) and, in 3D-network mode, each layer's layered position, rotation and shape (`layered`) and each node's layered position (`layered_position`): the session reloads in 3D-network mode and unticking it restores the layers.
- **Help rewritten.** One page per menu panel (Getting Started, Examples, File, 3D View & Navigation, Layout, Scene & Camera, Nodes, Edges, Layers, Style, Analyze, API, Cytoscape App, About); v2 panel screenshots, the separate FPS page and algorithms the app does not offer (Davidson-Harel, GEM, Star, Edge Betweenness) are gone.
- **Modern navigation icons.** The navigation table's PNG arrows are replaced by crisp line icons (SVG) — rotate and move arrows per axis, expand/collapse layers and nodes — keeping the original red/green/blue axis and yellow/purple group colours; they scale cleanly on high-DPI and touch screens.
- **Phones and tablets.** Touch gestures — one-finger orbit, two-finger pinch zoom and pan, double-tap select, long-press node menu — each an undo step; panels open as a bottom sheet on screens up to 768px; the ☰ menu scrolls and closes after a choice; 44px touch targets; the navigation table starts closed, the navigator and footer step aside; rendering capped at 1.5× pixel ratio on touch devices. Playwright now runs phone (Pixel 7) and tablet (Galaxy Tab S4) projects.
- **NORMA-2.0 group layouts** (Karatzas et al. 2022). Layout → Group Layout: virtual node per group, group gravity and super nodes (NORMA's weights), on clusters, annotations or the layers themselves, with a layout inside each group, force strength and group size. Layers as groups lays the whole network out in 3D, then places each floor at its group, turns it to face outward and fits its nodes; sphere/cube/cloud layers get 3D local layouts. In 3D, Circle now means nodes on a sphere. API: `group_strategy`, `groups`, `group_local`, `group_force`, `group_scale` on `POST /api/layout`.
- **Export to NORMA.** File → More Exports → Send to NORMA: posts the network to a NORMA server's API (`/api/external`, default normaviz.org) with each layer as an annotation group and opens it; or copies a self-contained `#json=` link, or saves the payload JSON.
- **Reorganised menus.** The 12 flat tabs are grouped by task: Home · View ▾ (3D View, Scene & Camera, Performance) · File · Layout · Edit ▾ (Nodes, Edges, Layers, Style) · Analyze ▾ (Analysis, Data Tables) · Help. A new Analysis panel gathers node metrics, network statistics, layer comparison, Explore and Annotations & Enrichment (previously split across the Layouts and Node panels). On narrow screens the ☰ menu closes after choosing a panel.
- **Pseudo-alignment across layers.** Layouts → Cross-Layer Alignment: _Soft_ (blend toward full alignment), _Anchor to a reference layer_ (seed/pin each layer at its counterparts' positions — same node or inter-layer partners) and _Chain_ (each layer to the previous), with a 0–1 strength. Every multi-layer layout reports an inter-layer misalignment score (mean in-plane stretch of cross-layer links, % of layout size) to compare options. API: `alignment`, `alignment_strength`, `reference_layer` on `POST /api/layout`; response `misalignment`.
- **Style panel and colour-blind support.** New Style tab: colour, size and shape (now per node, with cylinder and tetrahedron) for selected nodes, all nodes or a layer; colour by layer, cluster, annotation or degree with palettes — a validated distinct palette, Okabe–Ito and cividis; edge colour and per-edge width for selected/all/intra/inter edges; channel palette. _Colour-Blind Friendly Mode_ (Okabe–Ito plus one node shape per layer, one undo step) and a protanopia/deuteranopia/tritanopia/achromatopsia preview. Node shape and edge width persist in sessions.
- **3D layers and a layer-free 3D mode.** Layer Actions → Layer Shape turns layers into Cube, Sphere or Cloud containers (or None); their nodes get 3D layouts inside, are kept inside the volume, and layers are spaced so volumes don't overlap. 3D layouts now run on any number of layers (per layer or across them). Scene Actions → _3D Network (ignore layers)_ stacks every layer into one 3D space and lays the whole network out in 3D; unticking restores the layers. Layer shapes persist in sessions (`container`).
- **Topological analysis.** _Network Statistics_ (Layouts panel) reports per layer and for the whole network: density, degrees, components, largest component, diameter, average path length, clustering, transitivity, assortativity and a degree-distribution chart (View Data, CSV download; `POST /api/network-stats`). Five new node metrics for scaling: Closeness, Eigenvector Centrality, PageRank, Coreness (k-core), Eccentricity.
- **Node search with modes and camera focus.** Node Actions → Search: exact, contains, starts-with, ends-with (suffix) and regular-expression matching, optional case sensitivity, a clickable result list, and an animated, undoable camera focus on the matches (or one clicked node).
- **Loading feedback and file checks.** A progress bar shows upload %, server-side checking and the scene build. Network, session and attribute files are checked in the browser first (size, type, TAB separation, required columns with a case hint, ragged rows, non-numeric weights), naming the line at fault; server errors appear as plain messages (not raw JSON), and the TSV parser reports the line of a bad weight, empty cell or missing channel.
- **Round floors.** Layer Actions → Floor Shape: square or disk, for the selected layers (or all). Nodes are pulled inside the disk, later layouts stay inside it, and the shape is saved in sessions (`floor_shape`).
- **More import and export formats.** Upload GraphML, GML, SIF and Cytoscape CX2 (by file extension; a `layer` node attribute defines layers), import public NDEx networks by UUID (`POST /api/import/ndex`). Export GraphML, CX2, MuxViz (zip), SVG, GLB (3D), PNG at 2048/4096/8192 px, and a WebM video of one scene rotation.
- **Exploration tools.** Node Actions → Explore: expand the selection 1 or 2 hops (within layers, or across layers through copies of the same node), select the shortest path between two selected nodes (may hop layers), and select nodes by description/annotation/URL text. Edge Actions: a weight-threshold slider hides edges and channels below a scaled weight.
- **Annotations, hulls and enrichment.** Node attribute files accept an `Annotation` column (`;`-separated terms), shown as pie-chart nodes; translucent convex hulls can outline clusters or annotation terms on each layer; _Enrichment of Selected Nodes_ (Node Actions) runs STRING functional enrichment (`POST /api/enrichment`, GO + pathways by default), selects a term's genes on click, and can turn the top terms into node annotations. Annotations persist in sessions.
- **Multilayer analytics.** New node-scaling metrics computed across the selected layers — Multiplex Degree, Participation Coefficient and Multilayer PageRank — with an option to colour nodes by the metric; _Compare Selected Layers_ shows node and edge Jaccard overlap between layers as heatmaps in View Data (`POST /api/layer-similarity`); new _Leiden_ clustering, which runs as multiplex Leiden (`leidenalg`) with the align-across-layers scope. New backend dependency: `leidenalg`.
- **Layer arrangements and restructuring.** Four new arrangements in Scene Actions — Helix, Sphere, Grid and By Inter-Layer Links (a force layout of whole layers, so heavily linked layers sit together). Layer Actions gets _Split Selected Layer by Cluster_ (each cluster of a clustered layer becomes its own layer), _Merge Selected Layers_ (copies of a node collapse, links between them go) and _Lift Nodes by Degree_. All undoable.
- **Import from PPI databases** (File panel, ported from NORMA). _STRING, evidence types as channels_ fetches a STRING network and splits each interaction into up to seven evidence channels weighted by their scores; _One database per layer_ puts STRING, IntAct and OmniPath in their own layers and links copies of the same protein across them. New `POST /api/import/string` and `POST /api/import/databases`; results go through the TSV parser, so the usual size limits apply. Upstream URLs and the timeout are configurable (`ARENA_STRING_API`, `ARENA_INTACT_PSICQUIC`, `ARENA_OMNIPATH_API`, `ARENA_DB_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`).
- **Single-layer networks.** A network with one layer loads without its floor (new "Show Layer Floors" checkbox in Layer Actions brings it back), and a "3D Layout (one selected layer)" option runs Fruchterman-Reingold, Kamada-Kawai, DrL, Random or Grid in 3D so nodes leave the plane (`dimensions: 3` on `POST /api/layout`, positions come back as `[y, z, x]`). Node depth already round-trips through sessions.
- **Align nodes across layers.** New "Align Nodes Across Selected Layers" layout scope (`scope: "coupled"` on `POST /api/layout`): the selected layers are merged by node name, laid out once, and every copy of a node gets the same in-plane position in each layer, so multiplex layers can be compared by eye. Works with clustering; topology scaling rejects it.
- **Weighted layouts with channel weights.** The Layouts panel has a "Use Edge Weights (and Channel Weights)" checkbox. When on, edge weights pull nodes together in Fruchterman-Reingold, Kamada-Kawai and DrL (not with clustering), and each channel in the channel list gets a weight box (0.1–10, default 1) that multiplies its edges' weights, so a multi-channel network can be laid out mainly by one evidence type. Off by default, so existing layouts are unchanged. `POST /api/layout` takes the new optional `use_weights` and `channel_weights` fields.

## [3.1.0] - 2026-08-10

### Added

- Network upload accepts a minimal 2-column edgelist (`SourceNode`, `TargetNode` only) — all nodes are spread out in a single default layer named `Layer1`. The optional `Weight` and `Channel` columns still apply. A downloadable example lives in the Help → Examples tab.

- Edge weight can now be shown as **edge thickness**, not only opacity. The Edge Actions panel replaces the "Edge Opacity By Weight" checkbox with a "Show Edge Weight As" radio — Nothing / Opacity / Width / Both — plus intra- and inter-layer width sliders for whichever property weight isn't driving. Sessions carry the choice as the independent `edgeOpacityByWeight` and `edgeWidthByWeight` booleans; files written before this default to opacity, so they render unchanged. Thickness needed `Line2` (instanced quads) because WebGL renders every line primitive at exactly 1px regardless of `linewidth`.

### Changed

- Channel curvature sliders reach much further — intra-layer 10–60 (was 10–20), inter-layer 1–30 (was 1–10) — so the channels of a multi-channel edge can be pulled well apart instead of running nearly parallel. Defaults are unchanged, so existing sessions render exactly as before.

### Fixed

- Edges no longer glow. Thick edges cover a large share of the screen, and bloom on all of them was blinding against dark backgrounds. Bloom is now selective (three's off-screen bloom-composer pattern) and reads from node spheres alone, so nodes still glow, edges and layer planes stay crisp, and the extra pass costs a few spheres rather than a second full scene.
- Curved channel edges rendered as dotted lines with beads at the curve points once thickness landed. `LineMaterial`'s `worldUnits` mode assumes a perspective camera — its fragment shader traces a view ray from the camera origin and discards anything farther than half a width from the segment, which under this app's orthographic camera discards along the whole segment. Widths are now screen-space, sized against a shared resolution uniform kept on the frustum size (so the numbers still mean world units), retargeted on resize and for the PNG export.
- Node colors rendered washed out/dark in the 3D scene compared to the 2D navigator. The bloom composer was blitting linear color straight to the sRGB canvas (missing `OutputPass`), and the ambient light was left at the pre-r155 intensity that physical lighting divides by PI.
